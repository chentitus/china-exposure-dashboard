Dependence and Exposure to China

Turns Chinese official finance amounts into “dependence”: relative to GDP, to external debt, to Western alternatives, and fiscal stress. Indicators fall into three concepts:Debt(already locked in), Dependence(seeking help now, no alternative sources, fiscal stress), Firms(Chinese commercial presence: private debt, CGIT investment and construction); all included by default; for each concept the sub-score is the mean percentile of the selected indicators, and the total = mean of sub-scores (concepts weighted equally);This ranks exposure; it does not predict positions on Taiwan.
