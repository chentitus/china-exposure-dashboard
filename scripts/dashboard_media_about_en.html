Method (phase 1: counts)

The targets are the top five countries by exposure score in each of seven regions (North America is excluded because it has no Chinese official-finance ties), 35 countries in all, fixed in scripts/media_targets.csv. Data come from the GDELT Global Knowledge Graph 2.0 (BigQuery public dataset gdelt-bq.gdeltv2.gkg_partitioned, query in scripts/media_gkg_query.sql): English-language originals from the listed source domains since 2026-01-01 whose location field (V2Locations) includes the target country, followed by inclusion rules: state-media articles must name the target country (including adjectives and aliases such as Lao, Kinshasa, Brazzaville) in the title, which drops round-ups that mention it only in passing; international-media and wire articles must also be tagged to China (V2Locations includes CH), i.e. reporting about China and that country. An article mentioning several countries is counted for each of them.

  • Mentions Taiwan: V2Locations includes Taiwan (TW) or V2Themes includes TAIWAN — this only means Taiwan appears in the text (one-China statements at the end of joint statements, sports line-ups and regional economic news all count), not that the article is about Taiwan or the country's position on Taiwan; telling these apart needs phase 2 full-text reading, or a first grading by GKG character offsets ("in title / several / single mention"). One China: the page title contains "one China" (GKG has no full text, so this is judged from titles only and is a lower bound). Mentions China: V2Locations includes China (CH).
  • Source groups: Chinese state media = Xinhua (xinhuanet.com / news.cn / english.news.cn), China Daily, People's Daily English (en.people.cn), Global Times; wires = Reuters (reuters.com), AFP copy (france24.com, barrons.com); international media = BBC, Guardian, Al Jazeera, DW, Euronews, SCMP, CNA, Straits Times, ABC (Australia), Bloomberg, NYT, The Diplomat, VOA, RFA.

Limitations

  • GDELT barely indexes reuters.com (about 5,000 articles site-wide in 2026) or apnews.com (none), and AFP has no news site of its own; wire copy appears in GDELT mostly through outlets that republish it (CNA, Straits Times, Euronews, ABC, etc.), hence the separate "international media" group as a substitute.
  • CGTN (cgtn.com) and the Foreign Ministry's English site (fmprc.gov.cn) are not indexed by GDELT; the ministry's regular press conferences can be scraped directly (phase 2).
  • Only English originals are counted (empty TranslationInfo); Xinhua's Chinese-language copy is not.
  • GDELT location tagging has errors (for example "Georgia" the state vs the country, "Jordan" as a personal name), so counts for small countries and shared place names can be too high or too low.
  • Phase 1 does not read content; more coverage does not mean a pro-China stance. Phase 2 can code articles that mention Taiwan with an LLM (topic, wording on Taiwan).
